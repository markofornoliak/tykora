# TYKORA — terms and usage notes

*Last revised: 5 November 2026 · A plain-language guide for an experimental open-source product, not a legal opinion.*

TYKORA is an experimental, free typing training application. Its code is released under the [MIT license](../LICENSE), which controls permitted use, copying and redistribution of the software. These notes explain product behavior and known risks; they do not replace mandatory consumer or privacy rights that apply where you live.

**Your learning data belongs with your browser profile.** There is no account-based cloud sync or guaranteed remote recovery. You are responsible for keeping private backup copies if you want to move your learning history. A browser update, storage eviction, accidental deletion or lost device may remove locally saved results.

**Results are for practice, not certification.** Words per minute, accuracy, bot races, teacher reports and course progress are learning estimates, not proof of identity or professional qualification. Offline reports and friend race outcomes are not independently verified and may be manipulated. Published outcomes do not guarantee future improvement.

**Be careful with others' content and personal data.** Only import material you are permitted to use and avoid entering secret or highly sensitive information into practice text or classroom assignments. Other participants should explicitly consent before you exchange WebRTC connection codes or learning reports.

**Experimental features can change.** P2P races can fail due to network restrictions; classroom hand-offs are manual; mobile accessibility and input performance are not yet fully verified across devices. Availability and local data retention cannot be guaranteed. Some source code branches are draft engineering experiments, not published features.

For a problem, safety concern or licensing question, open an issue on the repository without attaching personal backups or network credentials. See the [privacy guide](PRIVACY.md) for data behavior and [README](../README.md) for current measured limitations.
