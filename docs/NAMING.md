**Additional exact-name historical trademark records:** [Canadian Intellectual Property Office, TYKORA #1705756](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/1705756?wbdisable=true) shows a Class 5 pharmaceutical mark application as **abandoned** (not an active registration). These do **not** establish global clearance; independently query current EUIPO/TMview, DPMAregister, UK IPO and WIPO Madrid Monitor for classes 9, 41, 42 and any adjacent goods/services.

## Three alternate directions — suggestions only

These are **creative candidates, not cleared names**. None is approved for a code/product/domain switch. Conduct exact/fuzzy trademark, domain and handle checks before choosing.

1. **TypeHarbor** — calm, safe place to practice; communicates accessible local-first learning. Risk: descriptive English compound, protection and domain availability unverified.
2. **Typorae** — short international-looking coined word that stays near typing without reusing the exact TYKORA token. Risk: similarity to existing “Typora” ecosystem, requires full legal search.
3. **KeyOrbitra** — distinctive rhythm and progress metaphor, easier to build a visual identity around multilingual practice. Risk: pronunciation, memorability and every registry check remain unverified.

Avoid adopting Keylume, RhythmKey, Keystra, KeyWeave or Tapfolio merely because they sound usable: public searches already reveal software or other established usage, sometimes directly related to typing or keyboard skills.
